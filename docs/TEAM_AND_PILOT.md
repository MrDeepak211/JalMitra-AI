## Proposed pilot

- Target: 10–15 farmers in 1–2 selected villages in Parbhani district.
- Potential users: farmers with small holdings and/or shared irrigation resources, where this is confirmed during discovery.
- No participants or field sites are confirmed in this document.
- Obtain consent and landholder permission before collecting personal data, photos or installing hardware.
- Collect only necessary data and avoid publishing farmer-identifiable information.
